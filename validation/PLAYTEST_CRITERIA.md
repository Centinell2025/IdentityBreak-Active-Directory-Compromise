# Author Playtest Criteria — No Solutions

Before HTB submission, an independent tester should be able to:
- complete all 15 questions without hidden information;
- identify the proxy clock discrepancy from supplied artifacts;
- distinguish benign engineering/automation activity from the incident;
- explain why account attribution is limited;
- support or reject each breach-scope classification with evidence;
- distinguish staging from exfiltration;
- establish selective encryption from more than an extension change;
- identify evidence gaps and avoid overclaiming anti-forensic intent;
- propose evidence-preserving containment;
- finish without executing malware or attacking any live service.

Reject or revise any question that has multiple equally defensible answers unless the expected response explicitly accepts them.
