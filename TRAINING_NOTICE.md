# Training and Educational Use Notice

**IdentityBreak — Active Directory Compromise** is a fictional cybersecurity training scenario for technical education, senior DFIR practice, SOC analyst development, incident-response exercises, and forensic engineering.

No real company, customer, employee, credential, confidential record, domain, production IP address, or production incident is represented. All scenario data is fictional or synthetically generated.

The exercise is intended for analysis of the supplied evidence and authorized isolated laboratories only. It does not contain executable ransomware, credential-stealing malware, destructive payloads, or instructions to compromise third-party systems.

Students are expected to practice evidence preservation, timeline reconstruction, identity analysis, breach scoping, safe containment, recovery planning, and defensible reporting.
