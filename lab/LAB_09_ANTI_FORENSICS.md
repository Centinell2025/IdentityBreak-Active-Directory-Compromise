# LAB-09 — Anti-Forensics & Timeline Recovery

**Role:** Senior forensic engineer

## Objective
Reconstruct events when local evidence is incomplete.

## Tasks
- Identify the recorded log-clear event and resulting gap.
- Analyze the staging artifact deletion.
- Evaluate the timestamp metadata observation without overstating intent.
- Identify independent sources that survive the local logging gap.
- Rebuild the affected timeline using directory, network, file-system, and collection evidence.
- Label each conclusion by confidence.

## Deliverable
Recovered Timeline with columns for canonical time, event, source, corroboration, confidence, and limitation.

## Pass criteria
The student distinguishes evidence of deletion/manipulation from proof of attacker intent.
