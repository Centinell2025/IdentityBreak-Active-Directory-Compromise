# LAB-10 — Breach Scope & Senior Incident Response

**Role:** Incident commander / senior DFIR analyst

## Objective
Convert technical findings into a defensible enterprise response.

## Tasks
- Complete reports/BREACH_SCOPE_TEMPLATE.csv.
- Classify hosts, identities, and datasets into Confirmed Compromised, Potentially Exposed, Observed but Unconfirmed, or No Evidence of Compromise.
- Prioritize forensic preservation.
- Define identity containment and session revocation.
- Define network containment while preserving investigative visibility.
- Define server isolation and clean recovery sequencing.
- Identify monitoring required after restoration.
- Write an executive statement on identity compromise, breach status, exfiltration confidence, encryption impact, and unresolved evidence gaps.

## Deliverable
Senior Incident Decision Package: breach-scope matrix, containment plan, recovery sequence, evidence requests, and executive summary.

## Pass criteria
Actions are evidence-based, proportionate, preserve evidence, and clearly separate confirmed findings from unresolved questions.
