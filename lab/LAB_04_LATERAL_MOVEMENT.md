# LAB-04 — Lateral Movement Reconstruction

**Role:** DFIR incident investigator

## Objective
Reconstruct only the lateral movement supported by evidence.

## Tasks
- Map authentication from WIN-FIN-04 to DC-01, FILE-01, APP-01, and DC-02.
- Correlate authentication records with network flow observations.
- Identify protocols represented by the supplied ports.
- Distinguish a proven network connection from a proven interactive action.
- Compare suspicious movement with legitimate AUT-01 and ADMIN-01 activity.
- Draw a confirmed-path diagram and a separate hypothesis diagram.

## Deliverable
Two diagrams: **Confirmed Evidence Path** and **Unconfirmed / Investigative Hypotheses**.

## Pass criteria
No inferred hop may be presented as confirmed without supporting evidence.
