# Senior Lab Objectives

IdentityBreak may be reproduced in an isolated training lab using Windows evaluation systems, Cisco-compatible lab networking, and a Kali analyst workstation, but the Sherlock evidence does not require malware execution.

Students should practice:
1. Evidence acquisition and hashing.
2. Windows / Active Directory event correlation.
3. Cisco/network flow interpretation.
4. File-system timeline reconstruction.
5. Data-breach scoping.
6. Safe analysis of simulated encryption effects.
7. Detection of log gaps and possible anti-forensics.
8. Timeline normalization.
9. Evidence-preserving containment.
10. Executive reporting with confidence levels.

The lab must use fictitious identities, disposable files, isolated networks, and harmless simulations only. Never reproduce this exercise against production infrastructure or systems without explicit authorization.
