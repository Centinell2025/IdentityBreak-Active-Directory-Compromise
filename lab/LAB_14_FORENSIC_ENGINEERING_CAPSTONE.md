# LAB-14 — Forensic Engineering Capstone

**Role:** Senior DFIR lead

## Mission
Investigate IdentityBreak from evidence intake through executive decision without using the simplified narrative as your primary source.

## Required work
1. Establish evidence provenance and limitations.
2. Normalize time across sources.
3. Reconstruct endpoint and identity sequence.
4. Establish confirmed lateral movement.
5. Analyze AD and file-share modifications.
6. Demonstrate or reject sensitive-data staging.
7. Assess external transfer evidence.
8. Determine selective encryption scope.
9. Reconstruct activity across the log gap.
10. Complete breach-scope classifications.
11. Design evidence-preserving containment and recovery.
12. Produce an executive breach statement with confidence.

## Deliverables
- Evidence register.
- Master UTC timeline.
- Attack/incident path diagram.
- Data-flow diagram.
- Breach-scope matrix.
- Technical findings report.
- Executive summary.
- Additional-evidence request.

## Senior grading standard
A high-quality submission is reproducible, distinguishes facts from inference, acknowledges contradictory or absent evidence, and does not overstate exfiltration or attribution.

No malware execution or unauthorized access is part of this capstone.
