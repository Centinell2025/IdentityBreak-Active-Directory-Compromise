# LAB-02 — Windows Endpoint Triage

**Role:** Senior endpoint forensic analyst

## Objective
Determine whether WIN-FIN-04 warrants escalation without treating every PowerShell event as malicious.

## Tasks
- Establish normal user activity immediately before the anomaly.
- Reconstruct process lineage around the suspicious execution.
- Compare WIN-FIN-04 with benign PowerShell activity on WIN-ENG-09 and AUT-01.
- Identify discovery-related activity.
- Correlate endpoint observations with the first relevant DNS/network records.
- Assign confidence to the hypothesis that WIN-FIN-04 is the initial investigated endpoint.

## Deliverable
Endpoint Triage Memo with a five-to-ten-event timeline, competing explanations, and collection requests for evidence not supplied.

## Pass criteria
The analyst separates unusual behavior from confirmed compromise and explains why process/user context is not human attribution.
