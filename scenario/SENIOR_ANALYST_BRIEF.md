# Senior Analyst Brief

You are the senior DFIR analyst assigned after initial SOC triage. Management wants an immediate answer to a simple question: **Was sensitive information stolen?**

Do not provide a simple answer until the evidence supports it.

Your responsibilities are to reconstruct the incident, identify what is confirmed, identify what remains uncertain, preserve evidentiary integrity, and communicate the difference between:
- compromised identity;
- unauthorized access;
- data discovery;
- data collection/staging;
- attempted transfer;
- confirmed disclosure;
- selective encryption;
- service impact.

## Required deliverables
- Normalized timeline.
- Answers to all 15 questions.
- Breach-scope matrix.
- Incident report.
- Evidence gaps and additional collection requests.
- Containment/recovery priorities.

A technically plausible story is not enough. Findings must be reproducible from the supplied artifacts.
